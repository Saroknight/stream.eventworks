Streamroom Live for Linux

For Arch and Arch-based distros (CachyOS, EndeavourOS, Manjaro). Encodes on your graphics card: VA-API on AMD and Intel, NVENC on NVIDIA. Works on Wayland (KDE, GNOME, Hyprland) and X11.

curl -L -o streamroom-live-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst https://stream.eventworks.click/api/app/download/linux && sudo pacman -U streamroom-live-x86_64.pkg.tar.zst
Free · installs with pacman, along with the GStreamer pieces it needs
1

Paste it in a terminal

Copy the command above, paste it into a terminal, press Enter, and type your password when asked. pacman installs the app and what it needs.

2

Pick your screen once

The first time you go live, your desktop asks which screen or window to share. Pick it and click Share. The app remembers it; to pick again, choose Ask every time under Screen in the app's Settings.

3

Sign in once, then use the app

In any room, click Go Live, then Open Streamroom Live (allow your browser to open it). You're live, and the app stays signed in: next time, start or join rooms right from the app.

Does it update itself?

Yes. When a new version is out, the app shows an Update now button. It never updates in the middle of a stream. On Linux it asks for your password, since pacman installs the update.

How do I sign the app out?

In the app, click ⋯ at the top, then Sign out of this PC. The admin can also sign it out from the admin page.

How do I stop streaming?

Click Stop streaming in the app, or right-click the red Streamroom Live icon near the clock and choose Stop streaming. Closing the window keeps you live, so you can play without it in the way.

Something's wrong

In the app, click Copy diagnostics at the bottom and send it to whoever runs this Streamroom.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Streamroom Live → Uninstall. Linux: sudo pacman -R streamroom-live.
