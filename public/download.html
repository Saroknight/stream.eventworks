3

Go live from any room

In a room, click Go Live, then Open the app. Allow your browser to open Streamroom Live, and you're streaming.

Does it update itself?

Yes. When a new version is out, the app shows an Update now button. It never updates in the middle of a stream.

How do I stop streaming?

Click Stop streaming in the app, or right-click the red Streamroom Live icon near the clock and choose Stop streaming. Closing the window keeps you live, so you can play without it in the way.

Something's wrong

In the app, click Copy diagnostics at the bottom and send it to whoever runs this Streamroom.

How do I uninstall it?

Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Streamroom Live → Uninstall.
